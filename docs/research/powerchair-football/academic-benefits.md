## ⭐ 주 근거 — Power Wheelchair Adaptive Team Sport Involvement (2025)

**서지**: *Research Quarterly for Exercise and Sport*, 2025.
DOI [10.1080/02701367.2025.2469910](https://doi.org/10.1080/02701367.2025.2469910) ·
전문 [PMC12310194](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12310194/)

**설계**: 다중방법 질적 사례연구. 전동휠체어 사용 성인 **10명**(미국 5·스칸디나비아 5),
파워사커와 볼트하키. 질적 인터뷰 + WHOQOL-BREF 삶의 질 설문 + 경기 중 심박수 측정.

⚠️ **표본 10명이다.** 인과("이 종목을 하면 삶의 질이 올라간다")로 읽으면 안 되고,
"이 선수들에게서 이렇게 측정됐다" 까지가 이 연구가 말하는 것이다.

### 네 가지 주제

> "Four qualitative themes described adults with disability experiences: **Community; Personal
> gain; Competition; and Barriers.**"

**① 공동체(Community)**
> "adaptive team sports provided an outlet for participants for establishing connections with
> people with similar life experiences. Our participants indicated that playing adaptive team
> sports resulted in **lasting friendships, extending beyond the sport**, opportunities for
> **mentoring others**, and for **advocating** for adaptive team sports."

**② 개인의 성장(Personal gain)** — 세 갈래로 나뉜다(정신·신체·정서).
> "Mental benefits were attributed by participants to **the challenge of the sport and
> competitive strategy**."

선수 인용:
> "It gives you a competitive edge, the ability to push yourself a little farther, physically,
> and emotionally. I think a lot of it's **more emotional than physical benefits**. I don't know
> how much exercise you can get from just the joystick, right? But there is emotional gain in
> that sense." (Player 2)

> "Volt means everything to me. A short summary would be, it's **my motivation for getting
> through the week**." (스칸디나비아 선수)

**③ 경쟁(Competition)**
> "playing adaptive team sports was **freeing and empowering**, physically challenging, and that
> they could 'feel the motion' of the sport."

**④ 장벽(Barriers)** — 아래 별도 절.

### ⭐ 삶의 질 수치 (WHOQOL-BREF)

> "our participants' WHOQOL-BREF **physical scores of 76.4 (SD = 9.09)** which were higher than
> those of people with neurodegenerative disorders; **57.3 (SD = 17.9)**; in people SCI;
> **58.5 (SE = 9.6)**; and **general population 73.5 (SD = 18.1)**."

| 집단 | 신체 영역 점수 |
|---|---|
| **이 연구의 선수들** | **76.4** |
| 신경퇴행성 질환자 | 57.3 |
| 척수손상인 | 58.5 |
| 일반 인구 | 73.5 |

즉 **같은 질환군보다 높았고, 장애가 없는 일반 인구보다도 높았다.**
⚠️ 네 도메인 평균은 60점이고, **사회관계 영역은 오히려 낮았다**(성생활 만족 47.5).
논문 자신이 그 비대칭을 적는다 — 한쪽만 인용하지 말 것.

### ⭐ 실제로 운동이 된다

> "average heart rate was **significantly higher** in the five US participants during scrimmage
> than at rest. This finding validates participants' expression of fatigue and physical effort…
> **The effort required to balance and position oneself in a PWC while playing a scrimmage game
> results in higher heart rate than while resting.**"

"조이스틱만 움직이는데 무슨 운동이냐"에 대한 직접적인 반증이다.
논문은 측정 조건상 **차이가 과소평가됐을 가능성**까지 적는다.

### 남은 장벽

> "participants articulated challenges related to adaptive team sports that concerned
> **transportation, lack of teams, ableist attitudes, team management, and having enough
> volunteers, equipment, and court space**."

선수 인용:
> "We need to figure out a way to have equipment available and good equipment that is not
> broken" (Player 10) · "You need coaches that know the rules. You need **referees** who know the
> rules" (Player 6) · "Getting more people to play, **making more teams** so we can play against
> each other" (Player 1)

📌 **이 앱에 직접 닿는 대목**: 심판·코치가 규칙을 모르는 것이 선수들이 스스로 꼽은 장벽이다.

### 사회적 함의 (논문의 "What does this article add?")

> "People with disabilities may experience **loneliness, low perceived social support, and social
> isolation at higher rates** than people without disabilities (Emerson et al., 2021)."

> "**Better quality of life** for adults with complex disabilities, who use wheelchairs **is
> closely related with their reintegration into social environments** (Filipcic et al., 2021)."

> "Often, adults with disabilities **learn about adaptive team sports from their health care
> practitioners**, thus health care practitioners should have knowledge of local programs to
> which they can refer participants. **This knowledge can increase equitable access to sports
> for adults with disabilities.**"

## ⚠️ 보조 — Jeffress & Brown (2017)

**서지**: Michael S. Jeffress & William J. Brown, "Opportunities and Benefits for Powerchair
Users Through Power Soccer", *Adapted Physical Activity Quarterly* **34(3)**, 2017.
(같은 저자의 단행본 *Communication, Sport and Disability: The Case of Power Soccer*, Routledge 2017)

**설계**: 미국 파워사커 선수 **34명**(여 11·남 23) 참여관찰 + 심층면접, 2007~2013.

**초록의 결론**: 참여가 **임파워먼트 증대**, **사회자본 획득**, 그리고 경쟁 스포츠를 하고 싶다는
욕구의 충족과 **독립의 기회**를 포함한 심리사회적 이익을 준다.

⚠️ **본문 미확보**(Human Kinetics 페이월, HTTP 403). 위 결론은 초록·검색 요약 수준이다.
존재와 서지는 PMC12310194 의 참고문헌이 교차 확인해 준다(본문에서 `Jeffress & Brown, 2017` 로 인용).
**앱에는 "임파워먼트·사회자본" 이라는 개념 틀까지만 쓰고 수치를 붙이지 말 것.**
